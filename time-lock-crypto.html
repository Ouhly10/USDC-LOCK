How this differs from a regular wallet

  • A wallet lets you move funds any time — a time lock removes that option until the date you chose.
  • The rule is enforced by code on Polygon, not by a policy that can change.
  • You keep full custody at every step; only the smart contract ever holds the locked balance.
  • Works for USDC, USDT and POL, with Monitor, Top Up and Withdraw tabs in the same app.

Getting started

Connect MetaMask, Trust Wallet or Coinbase Wallet, choose an amount and unlock date, and confirm the approval and lock transactions. Start on the Polygon Amoy testnet first if you want to see the flow before using real funds.
