Set your own locktime, on-chain

Locktime is simply the date and time your funds unlock. With usdtimelock.com you pick that exact moment when you create a lock, and a smart contract on Polygon enforces it — no early access, no exceptions, no admin key.

Set a locktime →

Why the locktime is trustworthy

  • The unlock date lives inside the smart contract, not a database someone could edit.
  • Every lock, top-up and withdrawal is visible on Polygonscan.
  • Works with USDC, USDT and POL from the same tool.
  • No installation — everything runs in your browser via MetaMask, Trust Wallet or Coinbase Wallet.

Choosing a good locktime

Pick a date tied to a real goal — a trip, a tax payment, an emergency fund you don't want to touch for a year. Because the locktime can't be shortened once set, treat it as final and start with a smaller test lock if you're unsure.
